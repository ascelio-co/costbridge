Verified Procore / Buildertrend → QuickBooks sync. In research.

I'm building a sync between construction management software (Procore, Buildertrend) and QuickBooks — specifically the job costing layer that breaks at month-end. Before I build it, I want to make sure I understand the problem correctly.

The problem (as I understand it)

  • Cost codes don't map 1-to-1 between Procore/Buildertrend and QuickBooks, so costs land in wrong accounts or fail to sync. The "Cannot use cost code more than once" error is common.
  • Two-way sync causes duplicate entries and ghost transactions. (Manual entry in QuickBooks while a sync is pending makes it worse.)
  • Change orders in draft status silently fail to sync. They just don't show up in QuickBooks until approved — sometimes not even then.
  • WIP reconciliation breaks at quarter-end because the two systems treat retainage and timing differences differently.
  • Payroll sync gaps — timecards and certain prime contract data aren't supported by existing integrations.

If you're hitting any of these, I'd like to hear about it. I'm trying to understand the shape of the problem before I build the wrong thing.
